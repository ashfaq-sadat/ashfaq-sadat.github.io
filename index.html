About Research Publications Teaching Code Background Contact

I study what microplastics and everyday chemical contaminants do to fish, and what that means for the rivers of Bangladesh and the people who eat from them.

I am a PhD researcher in the Department of Fisheries Management at Bangladesh Agricultural University, supervised by Professor Kizar Ahmed Sumon and supported by a University Grants Commission PhD scholarship. Most of my laboratory work uses Nile tilapia, from larvae through to adults, and I pair it with field sampling in rivers and estuaries.

Before the PhD I worked on post-harvest fish quality in the haor wetlands and led GIS survey teams at marine fish landing centres in Cox's Bazar. I do much of my own data analysis in R, and I teach research methods and data skills through Nexalyze Lab.

Research

Microplastics in fish and rivers

How much plastic ends up in water, sediment and fish, which polymers dominate, and how temperature and co-pollutants change the harm.

Pharmaceuticals and personal care products

Toxicity of compounds such as triclosan and ivermectin to early life stages and juveniles of farmed fish.

Risk assessment and One Health

Turning contaminant measurements into exposure and risk estimates for ecosystems and for consumers.

Fisheries and wetland ecology

Fish diversity in the haor floodplains, pressures on Sundarbans fisheries, and algal blooms along the Bay of Bengal coast.

What I am working on now

  1. Microplastics and pharmaceuticals from river to estuary My doctoral project. It follows microplastics and triclosan from the Old Brahmaputra River down to the Meghna estuary and asks what risk they pose along the way.
  2. Combined toxicity in Nile tilapia Exposure experiments on larvae and juveniles with polyethylene microplastics together with triclosan or ivermectin, to see whether the mixtures behave differently from each pollutant alone.
  3. What riverside communities know about plastic pollution A knowledge, attitudes and practices survey along the Old Brahmaputra in Jamalpur, Mymensingh and Gazipur.

Teaching and outreach

Nexalyze Lab

I founded Nexalyze Lab to make research methods easier to reach for Bangladeshi students. It publishes short explainers in Bengali on statistics and study design, and runs courses for students and early-career researchers.

Analysis support

I help colleagues and students with survey design, data cleaning and statistical analysis in R, from questionnaire to finished figures.
